CITATION
========

SolarWindPy is archived on Zenodo under the DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.17042839 <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17042839>`_.

This is Zenodo's concept DOI. It identifies every SolarWindPy release and
resolves to the most recent one. Each release also has its own version DOI,
listed under "Versions" on the Zenodo record. The version DOI identifies the
exact release you used.
