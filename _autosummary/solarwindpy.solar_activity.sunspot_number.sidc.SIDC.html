calculate_extrema_kind()[source]

Determine whether an SSN observation belongs to a cycle Minimum or Maximum.

Min: ssn < cycle mean Max: ssn >= cycle mean

Labels for the extremum kind include indicators for which cycle the extremum belongs to. These can be easily stripped with string manipulations, but are difficult to calculate after the fact.

plot_on_colorbar(cax, t0, t1, vertical_cbar=True)[source]

Plot SSN on the color bar.

The sunspot numbers are scaled onto the colour bar’s value axis over [0, top], where top is the window’s peak SSN rounded up to the next multiple of 100 and never less than 100, so the line always stays inside the scale. Major ticks are labelled 0, top / 2 and top; minor ticks fall every 25.
