solarwindpy.instabilities.verscharen2016

Instability thresholds from Verscharen et al. (2016).

The empirical fits of [VCKQ16] (doi:10.3847/0004-637X/831/2/128) are implemented to evaluate when a plasma becomes unstable in the (beta, R) plane.

References

[VCKQ16]

Daniel Verscharen, Benjamin D. G. Chandran, Kristopher G. Klein, and Eliot Quataert. Collisionless isotropization of the solar-wind protons by compressive fluctuations and plasma instabilities. The Astrophysical Journal, 831(2):128, November 2016. URL: http://dx.doi.org/10.3847/0004-637X/831/2/128, doi:10.3847/0004-637x/831/2/128.

Module Attributes

insta_params

Fit parameters a, b and c of each instability threshold (AIC, FMW, MM, OFI), indexed by growth rate as the exponent of gamma / Omega_p = 10^-4, 10^-3 and 10^-2.

Functions

Return the anisotropy threshold for a given beta.

Classes
